Assumptions and limits

  • Sabine formula, RT60 = 0.161 V / A, room treated as diffuse. Good for rooms with moderate absorption; over-estimates for very absorbent rooms.
  • Untreated absorption values are typical values for furnished rooms (assumed, not measured): living room with carpet, sofa and curtains; studio with a bare floor and hard walls.
  • Tile absorption is the model's band average over each octave, scaled by 0.8 for random incidence. Real rooms need a measured value for each tile.
  • Tiles are assumed to sit directly on the wall, with the 62 mm depth inside the tile. A gap behind the tile would raise low-frequency absorption.
  • Low frequencies (125–250 Hz) are not helped much by 62 mm-deep tiles. Bass traps or deeper absorbers are needed there.
